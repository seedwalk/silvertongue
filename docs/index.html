Silvertongue

Give your character a voice.

World of Warcraft · Burning Crusade Classic

Download Source on GitHub

You know what is happening. Silvertongue knows how to say it.

Click a target, pick Praise, read the line, click Say. Three seconds, and your character said something in character instead of nice heals bro.

Nothing reaches chat until you click to send it. Every button, key and menu in this addon fills a box and stops. You read the line first, every time.

IIt lives on the frames you already watch

No window to go and find. A small speech bubble sits on your portrait, on your target, on each party member, and on every row of the group browser. Clicking one opens a menu shaped like the game's own.

The channel buttons are the send — one click picks how it goes out and says it. You never choose a channel: opening the menu from your party means it goes to party chat. The refresh arrow says the same thing differently.

An enemy gets Respect on purpose. A worthy opponent deserves a word.

IIIt sounds like your character

A phrase is built in layers: a shared pool, then your class, your race, your faction, then the class and race of whoever you are talking to. Nobody wrote “undead mage” — the undead layer and the mage layer together already read as one.

IIIGestures, groups and the rest

It bows when it says thank you

Lines carry the matching emote, so your character actually bows, salutes or roars — and aimed at whoever you are speaking to, so it reads as Shamanolo bows before Gromkar. The checkbox shows you before it happens, because other people see it too.

It finds you a group, in character

A bubble in the Looking For Group window advertises you on the channel, opening with the shorthand people actually scan for:

LFG Scarlet Monastery — orc rogue, 36. Ready when you are.

Every row of the browser grows a bubble too. It reads the listing — who posted it, what they are running, what they are short of — and offers the roles they actually need and your class could fill. There is no “ask what they need”: the game already published it.

It is yours to edit

/silvertongue opens the library: every line this character can say, and where each one came from. Write your own, drop the ones you dislike, change a gesture, or rearrange any menu. Changes are stored as a diff, so new phrases in later versions still reach you.

You choose who a change applies to — all your characters, your race, your class or your faction. Dropping a line uses whichever of those the line already belongs to.

IVInstall

  1. Download the zip and unzip it. You should have a folder called Silvertongue.
  2. Put that folder in your AddOns directory: 
    World of Warcraft/_anniversary_/Interface/AddOns/Silvertongue/
  3. Restart the game. Not /reload — new addons are only found at launch.
The one thing that goes wrong: you should end up with AddOns/Silvertongue/Silvertongue.toc. If unzipping left an extra folder in between — AddOns/Silvertongue-rc-0.1.0/Silvertongue/ — the game will not find it. Move the inner folder up one level.

There is nothing to configure. It works on any character the moment it loads, and /silvertongue opens it. To put it on a key, use Esc → Key Bindings → Silvertongue.

Which build to take

Releases tagged v0.1.0 are finished. rc-0.1.0 are candidates that pass the tests but have had less time in a real game. Both install identically.

Updating

Replace the folder. Your own phrases, your menu order and wherever you dragged things live in the game's saved variables rather than in the addon folder, so they survive.

VIf you want to add to it

The phrases are plain Lua tables in Silvertongue/RP/. Adding lines, a race, a class or a faction is data — no other file needs to change. Write a line as { "text", emote = "BOW" } to give it a gesture.

tests/run.sh runs three checks against the source with the game API stubbed: that the TOC and the file tree agree, that the phrase library holds together, and that nothing reaches chat except through an explicit send. They run on every push.

Everything is on GitHub, including the issue tracker if something is wrong or a line sounds off.
